# Batch-culture growth and the plateau

A microbial batch culture typically grows exponentially and then **plateaus** (stationary
phase). The baseline Monod growth model, fit to the exponential phase, predicts continued
growth, so a plateau is a *discrepancy* to explain. Several distinct mechanisms can stop
growth, and they are not distinguishable from the growth curve alone — only from how the
culture responds to a deliberate perturbation.
