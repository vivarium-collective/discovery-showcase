# Discriminating interventions (general principle)

A perturbation applied at the plateau discriminates the mechanisms by whether growth resumes:

- **Add substrate**: if growth resumes, the nutrient was limiting (substrate exhaustion).
- **Remove the accumulated byproduct** (e.g. medium exchange holding substrate): if growth
  resumes, an inhibitory byproduct was the cause.
- **Dilution control**: matched handling; a confounded control (lowers density, substrate and
  byproduct together).

A single arm cannot separate all three mechanisms; a density cap resumes under neither
substrate-addition nor byproduct-removal. Choose interventions to fully discriminate.
