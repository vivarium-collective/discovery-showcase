# Candidate mechanisms that stop batch growth

- **Substrate exhaustion** — the limiting nutrient is consumed; growth stops when it runs out.
- **Byproduct inhibition** — an inhibitory metabolite (e.g. an organic acid) accumulates and
  suppresses growth even while substrate remains.
- **Density / quorum cap** — a density-dependent limit (space, quorum signalling) halts growth
  with substrate still plentiful and no inhibitory byproduct.

These are competing hypotheses; the data do not say which is operative.
